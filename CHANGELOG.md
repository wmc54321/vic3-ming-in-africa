# Changelog

All notable public releases are recorded here.

## 0.1.2

Completed the custom-law unlock chains and refined Great Ming's opening military technology.

- Replaced the unreliable focused cooperative-law compatibility file with documented same-name Victoria 3 1.13 overrides for Anarchy, Collectivized Agriculture, and Command Economy.
- Completed the vanilla reverse-reference and unlock chains for People's Capital Cooperation and Revolutionary Committees, including Factory Councils.
- Gave Great Ming the Line Infantry technology while retaining its mixed opening army of 22 line-infantry and 44 irregular-infantry battalions; wider modernization remains a player decision.
- Updated compatibility notes and release validation for the expanded vanilla law-file overrides.
- Declared compatibility with Victoria 3 1.13.x; a new campaign and a clean playset remain strongly recommended.

## 0.1.1

Expanded the Two Chinas route, chartered-company system, opening population model, and late-game political variants.

- Split national reconstruction from outward administration by adding the dedicated "Administer the Four Quarters" journal, with old-save repair for missing route journals.
- Added the Fusang and Southern Seas chartered companies, expanded company-region handling, introduced localized bridge cultures and legal homelands, and normalized existing companies in old saves.
- Added small worldbuilding-consistent Chinese administrative and ritual populations across Africa, generated from the source script.
- Added People's Capital Cooperation as a unification-winner economic-law variant, including its required law and production-method reverse references.
- Added Revolutionary Committees, its Factory Councils route, and the player-only Struggle Session character interaction with dedicated result events.
- Expanded dynamic country names, flags, coat of arms, localization, route UI text, compatibility notes, and implementation documentation.
- Rebalanced the 20-year opening modifiers to +1000% bureaucracy and -10 legitimacy at game start, both decaying linearly.
- Declared compatibility with Victoria 3 1.13.x; a new campaign and a clean playset remain strongly recommended.

## 0.1.0

Initial public release.

- Added the playable Great Ming state in Africa for the 1836 start.
- Added Ming-Qing political, subject, peaceful-unification, and war routes.
- Added reconstruction, capital relocation, and Chinese chartered-company content.
- Added Ming-specific cultures, laws, war goals, buildings, flags, and modifiers.
- Added Simplified Chinese and English localization.
- Declared compatibility with Victoria 3 1.13.x.
