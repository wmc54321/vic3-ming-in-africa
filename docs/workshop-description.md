## 简体中文

大明并未覆亡。它在非洲延续国祚，以尼罗河畔的应天府为都，统治一个横跨文化与大陆的帝国。1836 年，明清并立；你可以在非洲建立新的天下，也可以跨海争夺中华正统。

主要内容：

- 可玩的非洲大明 1836 年开局
- 明清战争、臣属与和平合流路线
- 迁都及重建国家日志链
- 中华属特许公司与边疆重组
- 专属文化、法律、战争目标、建筑和旗帜
- 完整简体中文及英文本地化

## English

The Great Ming never fell. It survived in Africa, ruling a continent-spanning and culturally diverse empire from Yingtian on the Nile. In 1836, Ming and Qing both claim China: build a new realm in Africa, or cross the seas to contest the Mandate.

Features:

- A playable African Great Ming in the 1836 start
- War, subject, and peaceful-convergence routes for the Two Chinas
- Capital relocation and national reconstruction journal chains
- Chinese chartered companies and frontier reorganization
- Unique cultures, laws, war goals, buildings, and flags
- Full Simplified Chinese and English localization

Version: 0.1.0

Supported game version: Victoria 3 1.13.x

Required DLC: None

Compatibility: this mod replaces state history and will generally conflict with mods that alter 1836 state ownership, starting borders, or state history. Start a new campaign and preferably use a playset containing only this mod.
