[h1]大明在非洲 / Ming in Africa[/h1]

[b]当前版本：0.1.1[/b]
[b]支持版本：Victoria 3 1.13.x[/b]
[b]所需 DLC：无[/b]

[i]大明并未覆亡。它在非洲延续国祚，以尼罗河畔的应天府为都，统治一个横跨文化与大陆的帝国。1836 年，明清并立：你可以在非洲重建新的天下，也可以跨海争夺中华正统。[/i]

[h2]主要内容[/h2]
[list]
[*]可玩的非洲大明 1836 年开局，拥有专属国号、旗帜、文化、法律、建筑、修正与本地化。
[*]“两个中华”完整路线：战争统一、臣属路线、共和制和平合流，以及失败方的“苦涩的和平”。
[*]“重建国家”与“经略四方”日志链：迁都北京或南京、整理边界，并将远方领土重组为中华属特许公司。
[*]八类中华属特许公司，覆盖非洲、内地、朝鲜、日本、西伯利亚、中亚、东南亚与婆罗洲；各地区拥有本地化中华桥梁文化、合法 homeland、动态国号和旗帜。
[*]更完整的开局世界观：非洲各州分布少量中华行政与礼仪人口，下埃及保留独立的南都人口结构。
[*]统一胜利方可采用“人民资本合作制”；委员会共和国路线可采用“革命委员会制”，并解锁工厂委员会与玩家专属“批斗”人物互动。
[*]完整简体中文与英文本地化，以及旧存档的日志和特许公司文化顺序修复。
[/list]

[h2]兼容性与使用建议[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]建议新开一局。[/b]虽然 0.1.1 会修复部分旧存档状态，但新增开局人口和 homeland 只能在新游戏中完整生效。
[*]本 mod 替换州历史，因此通常不兼容其他修改 1836 年州所有权、开局国土或州历史的 mod。
[*]本 mod 同名覆盖 [code]common/laws/00_labour_associations.txt[/code]，用于扩展工厂委员会条件；会与其他修改该文件的 mod 冲突。
[*]报告问题前，请先在只启用本 mod 的播放集中复现。
[/list]

[h1]English[/h1]

[b]Current version: 0.1.1[/b]
[b]Supported game version: Victoria 3 1.13.x[/b]
[b]Required DLC: None[/b]

[i]The Great Ming never fell. It survived in Africa and rules a continent-spanning, culturally diverse empire from Yingtian on the Nile. In 1836, Ming and Qing both claim China: rebuild a realm in Africa, or cross the seas to contest the Mandate.[/i]

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]A playable African Great Ming in the 1836 start, with unique names, flags, cultures, laws, buildings, modifiers, and localization.
[*]A complete Two Chinas route: military unification, subject play, republican peaceful convergence, and a Bitter Peace route for the defeated claimant.
[*]Rebuild the Nation and Administer the Four Quarters journals: relocate the capital, settle borders, and reorganize distant possessions into Chinese chartered companies.
[*]Eight chartered-company regions across Africa, China, Korea, Japan, Siberia, Central Asia, Southeast Asia, and Borneo, each with localized bridge cultures, legal homelands, dynamic names, and flags.
[*]A richer opening setting with small Chinese administrative and ritual communities across Africa and a distinct southern-court population in Lower Egypt.
[*]People's Capital Cooperation for the victorious unifier, plus Revolutionary Committees, Factory Councils, and a player-only Struggle Session interaction.
[*]Full Simplified Chinese and English localization, with old-save repair for route journals and existing chartered companies.
[/list]

[h2]Version 0.1.1[/h2]
[list]
[*]Added the Administer the Four Quarters journal and old-save route-journal repair.
[*]Added the Fusang and Southern Seas companies and completed all eight company regions, cultures, homelands, law inheritance, and free-state identities.
[*]Added generated Chinese starting populations across Africa and rebalanced the two 20-year opening modifiers.
[*]Added People's Capital Cooperation, Revolutionary Committees, Factory Councils compatibility, and the Struggle Session interaction.
[*]Expanded dynamic names, flags, coats of arms, route UI text, bilingual localization, and maintenance documentation.
[/list]

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]A new campaign is recommended.[/b] Version 0.1.1 repairs some old-save state, but new starting populations and homelands only apply fully to new games.
[*]The mod replaces state history and will generally conflict with mods that alter 1836 ownership, starting borders, or state history.
[*]The mod overrides [code]common/laws/00_labour_associations.txt[/code] to extend Factory Councils and will conflict with other mods that modify that file.
[*]Before reporting an issue, reproduce it in a playset containing only this mod.
[/list]
