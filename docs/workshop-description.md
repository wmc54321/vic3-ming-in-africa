[h1]非洲大明：Ming in Africa[/h1]

[b]当前版本：0.1.2[/b]
[b]支持版本：Victoria 3 1.13.x[/b]
[b]所需 DLC：无[/b]
[b]语言：简体中文 / English[/b]

[i]大明并未覆亡。它越过海洋，在非洲延续国祚，并以尼罗河畔的应天府为都。1836 年，明清并立、天下未定：你可以经营横跨非洲的旧帝国，在异乡重建一个新的中华秩序；也可以东渡重返故土，以战争、臣属关系或政治合流终结两个中华的时代。[/i]

[h2]你将扮演怎样的大明？[/h2]
非洲大明不是一个简单的强国开局。辽阔疆域、复杂人口与沉重行政成本同时存在；开局增益会在二十年内线性衰减，合法性压力则迫使你尽早建立能够长期运转的制度。大明已经掌握线列步兵技术，但继承军队仍然新旧混杂，后续整编、工业化与海外战略都由玩家决定。

[h2]核心玩法[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]两个中华：[/b]围绕明清正统展开完整路线，可通过统一战争、臣属安排或共和制政治趋同完成合流；失败方也拥有“苦涩的和平”结局。
[*][b]重建国家：[/b]处理非洲本土行政与边界问题，并在条件成熟后迁都北京或南京，决定这个流亡王朝最终把何处视为天下中心。
[*][b]经略四方：[/b]将远方领土重组为中华属特许公司。八类公司覆盖非洲、中华内地、朝鲜、日本、西伯利亚、中亚、东南亚与婆罗洲。
[*][b]在地化帝国：[/b]各公司拥有本地化中华桥梁文化、合法 homeland、动态国号与旗帜；非洲各州也分布少量中华行政和礼仪人口，下埃及保留独特的南都人口结构。
[*][b]专属政治制度：[/b]统一胜利方可选择“人民资本合作制”；委员会共和国路线可采用“革命委员会制”，并衔接无政府制、集体化农业、计划经济与工厂委员会等原版法律链。
[*][b]叙事与表现：[/b]包含专属国号、旗帜、纹章、文化、法律、战争目标、建筑、修正、日志、事件及完整中英文本地化，并提供旧存档的部分路线与公司状态修复。
[/list]

[h1]English[/h1]

[b]Current version: 0.1.2[/b]
[b]Supported game version: Victoria 3 1.13.x[/b]
[b]Required DLC: None[/b]
[b]Languages: Simplified Chinese / English[/b]

[i]The Great Ming never fell. It crossed the seas, survived in Africa, and now rules from Yingtian on the Nile. In 1836, Ming and Qing both claim China. Build a durable new realm in Africa, or sail east and end the age of Two Chinas through war, subordination, or political convergence.[/i]

[h2]What kind of Great Ming will you build?[/h2]
African Great Ming is not a simple power-fantasy start. Vast territory, a complex population, and severe administrative costs arrive together. Its opening modifiers decay linearly over twenty years, while legitimacy pressure rewards early institutional reform. Ming knows Line Infantry, but its inherited army remains mixed and uneven; reorganization, industrialization, and overseas strategy are left to the player.

[h2]Core Features[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Two Chinas:[/b] Resolve the rival claims through unification war, subject arrangements, or republican political convergence, with a Bitter Peace outcome for the defeated claimant.
[*][b]Rebuild the Nation:[/b] Stabilize the African realm, settle borders, and eventually move the capital to Beijing or Nanjing.
[*][b]Administer the Four Quarters:[/b] Reorganize distant possessions into eight Chinese chartered-company regions spanning Africa, China, Korea, Japan, Siberia, Central Asia, Southeast Asia, and Borneo.
[*][b]A localized empire:[/b] Companies receive regional Sinitic bridge cultures, legal homelands, dynamic names, and flags. Small Chinese administrative and ritual communities also give the African opening setting more history and texture.
[*][b]Unique political paths:[/b] The victorious unifier can adopt People's Capital Cooperation. A Council Republic can develop Revolutionary Committees, connected to Anarchy, Collectivized Agriculture, Command Economy, and Factory Councils.
[*][b]Narrative presentation:[/b] Unique names, flags, coats of arms, cultures, laws, war goals, buildings, modifiers, journals, events, and full Chinese-English localization, plus limited old-save repair for routes and companies.
[/list]
