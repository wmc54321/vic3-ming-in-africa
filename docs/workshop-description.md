[h1]非洲大明：Ming in Africa[/h1]

[b]当前版本：0.1.5[/b]
[b]支持版本：Victoria 3 1.13.x[/b]
[b]强制 DLC：无[/b]

[i]大明并未覆亡，而是在非洲延续国祚，以尼罗河畔的应天府为都。1836 年明清并立：你可以经营非洲帝国，也可以东渡故土，以战争、臣属或政治合流终结两个中华的时代。[/i]

[h2]核心玩法[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]两个中华：[/b]从专属开局目标选择大明或大清，通过统一战争、臣属安排或共和制政治趋同结束两朝并立；失败方也拥有“苦涩的和平”结局。
[*][b]重建国家：[/b]治理非洲并迁都；建设可叠加的两所国子监与广州、靖海海贸奇观。
[*][b]坤舆矿务：[/b]以南部非洲煤铁开发取得设局资格，统筹煤、铁、硫、铅、金五类矿业，并在公司繁荣后打造“龙印精铁”。
[*][b]经略四方：[/b]将远方领土重组为中华属特许公司。八类公司覆盖非洲、中华内地、朝鲜、日本、西伯利亚、中亚、东南亚与婆罗洲。
[*][b]在地化帝国：[/b]各公司拥有中华桥梁文化、合法 homeland、动态国号与旗帜；非洲各州分布少量中华行政和礼仪人口。
[*][b]专属政治制度：[/b]统一胜利方可选择“人民资本合作制”；委员会共和国路线可采用“革命委员会制”；法团国家或社会君主制路线可采用“国民训政制”，并通过轻量日志宣布或解除戒严、延期或恢复选举。两法还会固定改写24个代表州名，并使应天首都圈随政体切换名称。
[/list]

[h2]兼容性与开局建议[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]强烈建议新开一局。[/b]部分旧存档状态可以修复，但开局人口、homeland、科技和历史文件变更只有新游戏能够完整生效。
[*]本 mod 替换州历史，并同名覆盖南欧开局人口/建筑（用于修复克里特）及部分法律文件；会与修改相同内容的 mod 冲突。
[*]没有强制 DLC 要求；坤舆矿务总局本体可直接使用，其产业特许扩展与“龙印精铁”需要《Charters of Commerce》对应功能。
[*]报告问题前，请在只启用本 mod 的干净播放集中复现。
[/list]

[h1]English[/h1]

[b]Current version: 0.1.5[/b]
[b]Supported game version: Victoria 3 1.13.x[/b]
[b]Mandatory DLC: None[/b]

[i]The Great Ming never fell. It survived in Africa and rules from Yingtian on the Nile. In 1836, Ming and Qing both claim China: govern an African empire, or sail east and end the age of Two Chinas through war, subordination, or political convergence.[/i]

[h2]Core Features[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Two Chinas:[/b] Choose Great Ming or Qing from a dedicated starting objective, then end the rival claims through unification war, subject arrangements, or republican political convergence. The defeated claimant can pursue a Bitter Peace.
[*][b]Rebuild the Nation:[/b] Stabilize Africa; build stackable academies and maritime wonders in Lower Egypt, Beijing, and Guangdong.
[*][b]Mines of the Realm:[/b] Earn the Kunyu Directorate of Mines through early Coal or Iron development in Southern Africa, coordinate five strategic mining industries, and establish Dragon-Seal Bar Iron once the company prospers.
[*][b]Administer the Four Quarters:[/b] Reorganize distant possessions into eight Chinese chartered-company regions spanning Africa, China, Korea, Japan, Siberia, Central Asia, Southeast Asia, and Borneo.
[*][b]A localized empire:[/b] Companies receive Sinitic bridge cultures, legal homelands, dynamic names, and flags. Small Chinese administrative and ritual communities enrich the African setting.
[*][b]Unique political paths:[/b] The victorious unifier can adopt People's Capital Cooperation. A Council Republic can develop Revolutionary Committees, while a Corporate State or Social Monarchy can adopt National Tutelage and use a focused journal to declare or lift martial law, postponing or restoring elections. Both laws also apply fixed political names to 24 selected states, including Yingtian's capital region.
[/list]

[h2]Compatibility and Starting Advice[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]A new campaign is strongly recommended.[/b] Some old-save state can be repaired, but starting populations, homelands, technology, and history changes only apply fully to a new game.
[*]The mod replaces state history and overrides South European starting population/building history (to repair Crete) plus selected law files; it conflicts with mods changing the same content.
[*]No DLC is mandatory. The Kunyu Directorate itself remains available without DLC; its industry-charter extension and Dragon-Seal Bar Iron require the corresponding Charters of Commerce feature.
[*]Before reporting an issue, reproduce it in a clean playset containing only this mod.
[/list]
